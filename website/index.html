Curator
Changelog Source

See what Plex just added.

Curator is a Mac app for your Plex library. Import a disc and watch it land: the newest titles come first, anything from the last three hours is marked new, and every title shows exactly which file Plex will play.

Download Curator Free and open source. Requires macOS 26 and a Plex Media Server.

In the collection

  • Recently Added

    Refreshed every minute

    Every library, newest first, grouped by day. A banner appears when a title lands, and the menu bar keeps your last five imports one click away.

  • Search

    Titles, cast and crew

    Type a title, an actor or a director. When a match comes from the cast or crew, Curator says why: “Director: Joel Coen.”

  • The details

    Down to the file

    Artwork, summary, cast, when it was added, the video format, and the path and size of the file on your server. Open it in Plex with one click.

  • At home on a Mac

    Grid or list, keyboard first

    Arrow keys move through posters, Return opens a title in Plex, and Space shows a quick preview. Updates install themselves.

Set up in a minute

  1. Download Curator, open the disk image and drag Curator to Applications.
  2. Open Settings and enter your Plex server’s address, such as my-plex-mac or its IP address.
  3. Paste your Plex token. Settings walks you through finding it in Plex Web.

Posters come from your Plex server. Add a free TMDB key in Settings if you’d like TMDB’s artwork instead.
