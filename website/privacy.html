Privacy

Curator collects nothing about you and has no analytics. It talks to three places:

  • Your Plex Media Server, at the address you enter, to list and search your library.
  • TMDB, only if you add a TMDB key, to fetch posters and backdrops for titles in your library.
  • curator.sstools.co, to check for updates. The request identifies only Curator’s version; nothing about your library is sent.

Your server address, Plex token and TMDB key stay on your Mac, in a settings file only you can read.
